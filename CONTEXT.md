# Adventure Capital

Financial planning context for modeling startup growth, optimizing an accelerated growth plan, valuing the resulting cashflows, and producing business-facing financial reports.

## Language

**Acquisition**:
A new customer captured during a monthly planning period.
_Avoid_: Adquisition

**Adquisición**:
Spanish business-facing label for **Acquisition**.
_Avoid_: Adquisition

**Fixed Acquisition Period**:
The first twelve monthly periods where **Acquisition** is provided as an external assumption rather than chosen by the growth plan.
_Avoid_: Base year, historical period

**Planning Period**:
One month in the planning horizon.
_Avoid_: Quarter, year

**Service**:
A sellable offering with its own price, recurrence frequency, churn, and operating cost assumptions.
_Avoid_: Product, line item

**Service Cohort**:
Customers acquired for a specific **Service** during one **Planning Period**.
_Avoid_: Generic cohort, global customer cohort

**Active Client Pool**:
The surviving customers from prior and current **Service Cohorts** for a **Service** in a **Planning Period**.
_Avoid_: User base, baseline clients

**New Sale**:
A sale generated one-to-one by **Acquisition** in the same **Planning Period**.
_Avoid_: First purchase

**Service Price**:
The average price charged for one sold **Service**, currently constant across the planning horizon unless explicitly modeled otherwise later.
_Avoid_: Ticket when used ambiguously

**Revenue**:
Income generated by multiplying **Total Services Sold** by **Service Price** across services in a **Planning Period**.
_Avoid_: Cashflow, sales count

**Liquidity Policy**:
The rule that determines the minimum allowed cash position during the planning horizon.
_Avoid_: Cash formula, valuation constraint

**Operational Cost Floor**:
The minimum operating cost required to support an installed operational capacity step.
_Avoid_: Fixed plus variable cost

**Recurring Sale**:
A sale generated when surviving customers from a prior **Service Cohort** repurchase a **Service** according to that service's recurrence frequency.
_Avoid_: Subscription, renewal

**Repurchase Window**:
A precomputed eligibility point where a prior **Service Cohort** may generate **Recurring Sales** for a **Service**.
_Avoid_: Binary decision, recurrence variable

**Service Churn**:
The service-specific loss rate applied to a **Service Cohort** over time.
_Avoid_: Global churn, company churn

**Repurchase Rate**:
The percentage of eligible surviving customers in a **Service Cohort** that generate a **Recurring Sale** during a **Repurchase Window**.
_Avoid_: Conversion rate, global recurrence

**Total Services Sold**:
The sum of **New Sales** and **Recurring Sales** for a **Service** in a **Planning Period**.
_Avoid_: Total customers, active clients

**Annual Aggregation**:
A summary view that groups monthly **Planning Periods** by year without changing model granularity.
_Avoid_: Annual model, yearly period

**Standard Valuation Report**:
A Spanish client-facing report that presents valuation, financial planning, unit economics, and strategic narrative using model outputs plus document-specific narrative inputs.
_Avoid_: Notebook output, raw report dump

**Document YAML**:
A report-specific input file containing narrative and presentation data for one **Standard Valuation Report**, separate from model configuration.
_Avoid_: Model config, instance YAML

**Report Data Package**:
The intermediate render-ready data package that normalizes model outputs and **Document YAML** inputs for generating a **Standard Valuation Report**.
_Avoid_: Work document, final report

## Relationships

- An **Acquisition** belongs to exactly one **Service** and one **Planning Period**.
- A **Service Cohort** contributes surviving customers to the **Active Client Pool** for its **Service** after **Service Churn**.
- A **Recurring Sale** comes from a prior **Service Cohort** when a **Repurchase Window** applies and the **Repurchase Rate** is applied to surviving customers.
- Each **Acquisition** creates exactly one **New Sale** for the same **Service** and **Planning Period**.
- **Total Services Sold** equals **New Sales** plus **Recurring Sales** for one **Service** in one **Planning Period**.
- **Revenue** is calculated from **Total Services Sold** and **Service Price**.
- A **Liquidity Policy** constrains or observes cash availability across **Planning Periods**.
- An **Operational Cost Floor** applies to **Total Services Sold** through operational capacity steps.
- A **Service Price** is constant in the first refactor, but future models may make it period-specific.
- The **Fixed Acquisition Period** contains exactly twelve **Planning Periods**.
- An **Annual Aggregation** summarizes twelve **Planning Periods**.
- **Adquisición** and **Acquisition** refer to the same business concept in different languages.
- A **Standard Valuation Report** is generated from a **Report Data Package**.
- A **Report Data Package** combines model output artifacts with one **Document YAML**.
- A **Document YAML** does not define optimization assumptions.

## Example dialogue

> **Dev:** "When the model shows **Adquisición** in month 6, should code call that `adquisition`?"
> **Domain expert:** "No — the English term is **Acquisition**; **Adquisición** is the Spanish output label. Annual views are only **Annual Aggregations** of monthly **Planning Periods**."

## Flagged ambiguities

- "adquisition" was used as an English spelling for **Acquisition** — resolved: use **Acquisition** in English and **Adquisición** in Spanish outputs.
- "cohort" can mean a global customer cohort or a service-specific cohort — resolved for current model: use **Service Cohort** because acquisition, churn, recurrence, and sales are indexed by **Service**.
- "binary variable" was used for recurrence timing — resolved: recurrence timing is a precomputed **Repurchase Window**, not a solver decision variable.
- "churn" can mean service-specific churn or global customer churn — resolved: current model uses **Service Churn** only, applied through service cohort survival.
